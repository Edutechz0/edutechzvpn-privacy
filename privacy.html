Privacy Policy for EdutechzVPN

Effective Date: September 27, 2026

At EdutechzVPN, your privacy and digital security are our highest priorities. This Privacy Policy outlines our strict "No-Logs" policy and explains how we handle the information when you use our Android application.

1. Strict No-Logs Policy

EdutechzVPN is built on a fundamental commitment to privacy. We do not collect, log, store, or share any of the following data:

  • Your browsing history or websites visited
  • Your DNS queries
  • Your incoming or outgoing traffic data
  • Your original IP address
  • The VPN servers you connect to

Because we do not store this data, we cannot share it with any third parties, advertising networks, or government entities under any circumstances.

2. Data We DO Collect (And Why)

To provide and maintain our service, the app may locally process minimal diagnostic data directly on your device:

  • Hardware ID (HWID): If you use hardware-locked configurations, the app securely hashes your device identifier locally to verify config permissions. This hash is never transmitted to our servers for tracking purposes.
  • Crash Logs (Optional): If the app crashes, anonymous diagnostic logs may be generated by the Android OS to help us fix bugs. These are only shared if you explicitly opt-in via Google Play Services.

3. Android VpnService API

EdutechzVPN utilizes Android's built-in VpnService to create a secure, encrypted tunnel for your internet traffic. This is a core requirement for the app to function as a VPN. All traffic routed through this service is heavily encrypted using military-grade protocols (Xray, SSH, OpenVPN, Hysteria) before it ever leaves your device.

4. Third-Party Services

Our app may route your encrypted traffic through highly secure third-party server infrastructure (such as Cloudflare DoH or custom VPS nodes) depending on the configuration you use. However, these connections are heavily encrypted, ensuring your traffic cannot be read or intercepted by ISPs, network admins, or intermediate servers.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Effective Date" at the top.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer via the email provided on our Google Play Store listing.
